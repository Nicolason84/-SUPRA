Status: CANONICAL_PEOPLE_PROCESS
Authority: NICOLAS
Owner: D12 — People / RH & Organization
Principle: SMALLEST_CAPABLE_ORGANIZATION / EVIDENCE_BEFORE_HIRE / HUMAN_ACCOUNTABILITY

## 1. Purpose

Create a complete people operating system that works for:
- founder
- employees
- apprentices / interns where applicable
- contractors
- external providers
- advisors
- partners with operational responsibilities
- digital support workers

Digital workers are tracked for capacity and responsibility routing but are never classified as employees.

## 2. Required person / seat distinction

PERSON = real individual with evidence.
SEAT = organizational role that may be vacant.
ROLE = responsibility definition.
WORKER = human or digital execution capability.
AUTHORITY = explicit decision/action permission.

Never infer PERSON from SEAT.
Never infer EMPLOYMENT from WORK CONTRIBUTION.
Never infer LEGAL AUTHORITY from job title.

## 6. Sourcing

Allowed channels may include:
- direct network
- referrals
- specialized communities
- job boards
- schools / apprenticeships
- agencies
- contractors/freelancers
- strategic partners

SUPRA may:
- prepare role description
- research channels
- structure candidate pipeline
- summarize evidence
- prepare interview packs
- schedule when authorized

SUPRA may not:
- impersonate a human recruiter
- make binding employment offers
- discriminate using protected attributes
- make final hiring decisions

## 7. Selection process

Default lean process:

1. ROLE GATE
2. SHORT EVIDENCE-BASED SCREEN
3. PRACTICAL WORK SAMPLE WHEN RELEVANT
4. ROLE-SPECIFIC INTERVIEW
5. VALUES / OPERATING PRINCIPLES INTERVIEW
6. REFERENCE / LEGAL CHECKS ONLY WHEN JUSTIFIED AND LAWFUL
7. COMPENSATION / CONTRACT REVIEW
8. HUMAN DECISION
9. OFFER
10. ACCEPT / DECLINE LOG

Selection evidence should focus on:
- demonstrated capability
- quality of judgment
- reliability
- learning speed
- communication
- role-relevant execution
- respect for evidence, security and authority boundaries

## 18. People data privacy

HR data is NOT ordinary organizational knowledge.

Classification:
PUBLIC_ROLE_DATA
INTERNAL_ORG_DATA
CONFIDENTIAL_PEOPLE_DATA
RESTRICTED_LEGAL_HR_DATA

Rules:
- minimum necessary data
- role-based access
- provenance
- retention policy
- no uncontrolled propagation to general memory
- no training/learning reuse of confidential personal data unless explicitly authorized and appropriate

## 19. Offboarding

Trigger:
resignation / end of contract / role change / termination / provider end.

Checklist:
1. human/legal decision
2. effective date
3. work handover
4. asset return
5. access revocation
6. secret/key rotation where necessary
7. customer/partner transfer
8. records preservation
9. financial close
10. confidentiality/IP reminders
11. role/seat state update
12. lessons learned

No destructive deletion of company evidence.

## 24. Acceptance

PEOPLE_OS_V1_PASS when:
- roles and seats are separated
- real people are evidence-backed
- no digital worker is counted as staff
- every critical seat has accountability
- recruiting workflow exists
- onboarding/offboarding workflows exist
- access lifecycle exists
- performance is outcome-based
- skills are evidence-backed
- sensitive HR data has stronger privacy than general memory

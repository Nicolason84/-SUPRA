## 0. Core distinction

NOVA ERA must distinguish four different things:

1. LEGAL ORGANIZATION — legally appointed people and entities.
2. HUMAN ORGANIZATION — employees, contractors, advisors and partners.
3. OPERATING ORGANIZATION — departments, roles, responsibilities and workflows.
4. DIGITAL WORKFORCE — SUPRA capabilities, agents, workers, automations and models.

A digital worker is NEVER an employee, officer, legal representative, contractual signatory or accountable human.

Every delegated digital activity has:
- accountable_human
- delegation_scope
- allowed_actions
- prohibited_actions
- evidence_required
- human_gate_conditions
- rollback

## 4. Current human truth vs target organization

The existence of a department does not imply an employee exists.

Initial canonical assumption until reconciled with HR/legal records:
- Founder / President: HUMAN_STAFFED / authoritative human.
- Other roles: VACANT, FUTURE_HIRE, CONTRACTOR, EXTERNAL_PROVIDER or DIGITAL_SUPPORT unless evidence proves otherwise.
- SUPRA workers: DIGITAL_SUPPORT only.

Historical business-plan evidence is preserved as HISTORY, not silently treated as current staffing:
- first technician freelance or part-time
- founder focused on sales, prospecting, partnerships and development
- later technician/apprentice
- later commercial/customer role
- partial support outsourcing possible

Current staffing must be recovered from actual contracts/payroll/provider records before promotion.

## 10. Hiring gates

No hiring because "a company should have that role."

A human hire/contractor becomes justified when a measurable bottleneck persists after:
1. simplification
2. automation
3. reuse
4. process repair
5. temporary external support

Initial trigger examples to calibrate with real data:

### First Delivery / Technician capacity
Trigger candidates:
- founder delivery work repeatedly displaces revenue/product work
- service/installation backlog threatens commitments
- field workload exceeds sustainable capacity
- quality/rework indicates specialization is needed

### First Sales / Customer role
Trigger candidates:
- qualified pipeline exceeds follow-up capacity
- response/follow-up delays reduce conversion
- founder has repeatable offer + proven sales motion
- active customers create retention/support load

### First Engineering hire
Trigger candidates:
- production roadmap is blocked by sustained engineering capacity shortage
- maintenance burden materially slows commercial delivery
- security/reliability requires dedicated ownership

### First Finance/Admin support
Trigger candidates:
- invoicing/collection/admin consumes material founder capacity
- closing/compliance errors or delays appear
- transaction volume requires segregation/control

Every hire proposal must include:
problem
evidence
automation_attempted
alternatives
role
expected capacity gain
fully-loaded cost
revenue/risk impact
90-day outcomes
kill/continue gate

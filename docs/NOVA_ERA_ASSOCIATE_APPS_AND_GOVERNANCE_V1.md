## 5. Security

Every app session binds:
PERSON_ID
ASSOCIATE_ID
DEVICE_ID
ROLE_PROFILE
AUTHORITY_SCOPES
MFA_STATE
SESSION_EXPIRY
EVIDENCE

Associates cannot:
- self-grant banking rights
- self-increase card limits
- self-approve own expenses above policy
- alter cap table
- sign on behalf of company without explicit authority
- move company money without approved scope
- access private HR/legal data beyond authorization

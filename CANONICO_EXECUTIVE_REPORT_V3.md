## 3. PARADIGM SHIFT: V1 → V2 → V3

### V1 (Knowledge Graph)
```
The Graph represents reality
  → "What does the Graph contain?"
  → Storage, retrieval, projection
  → Static description of entities
```

### V2 (Constraint Information Graph)
```
The Graph demonstrates its own coherence
  → "Is the Graph coherent?"
  → "Why is it not?"
  → "What is the minimal fix?"
  → Dynamic validation of constraints
```

### V3 (Knowledge & Coherence Operating System)
```
The Graph learns from heterogeneous corpora
  → "What does the corpus contain?"
  → "Is the corpus internally coherent?"
  → "Where are the cross-document contradictions?"
  → "What are the systemic weaknesses?"
  → Automatic concept extraction, cross-document reconciliation,
    proof chain verification, and corpus-level coherence auditing
```

### Foundational Principle (V3)

> A corpus is coherent **iff** every concept has a unique, non-contradictory definition across all documents, every claim has a complete evidence chain, every constraint is consistent with all other constraints, and every relation respects the unified ontology.

## 5. KEY INNOVATIONS (V3)

### 5.1 Knowledge Graph Engine
Automatic extraction of concepts, principles, hypotheses, and evidences from heterogeneous document corpora. Cross-document resolution with similarity scoring and contradiction detection.

### 5.2 Unified Ontology
Complete mapping of UGIS ↔ ProofGraph ↔ INPI ↔ SUPRA concepts. 68 canonical concepts with 62 cross-concept relations. Equivalence classes, close concepts, contradictions, and hierarchy mappings.

### 5.3 Pattern Constraint Profiles
Every Pattern now carries a complete constraint set across all 15 categories: required/forbidden properties, relations, states, cardinality, lifecycle rules, transition rules, trust rules, evidence rules, governance rules, dimension rules, and invariants.

### 5.4 15 New Conflict Types (V3)
Beyond the 18 V2 inconsistency types, V3 adds: Identity, Semantic, Structural, Temporal, Evidence, Governance, Lifecycle, State, Constraint, Projection, Pattern, Proof, Ontology, Vocabulary, and Duplication conflicts — totaling 33 specific inconsistency types.

### 5.5 ProofGraph Fusion
Complete conceptual mapping between ProofGraph and CANONICO. Equivalence mappings (18), close concept mappings (12), constraint correspondences (8 new constraints from ProofGraph). Chain JSON mapping specification.

### 5.6 Cross-Corpus Root Cause Analysis
Extended backward chaining across document boundaries. Identifies document origins of conflicts, traces dependent documents, detects systemic weaknesses, and proposes minimal corrections with propagation simulation.

### 5.7 Full Corpus Audit
Systematic audit of the entire combined corpus. Identified 14 inconsistencies, 12 duplicates, 5 orphans, 6 missing relations, 5 weak dependencies, 5 unsubstantiated claims, 8 hypotheses without proof, and 3 proofs without hypotheses.

### 5.8 Coherence Scoring
Corpus-level health score: **0.87 / 1.00 (HEALTHY)** with prioritized correction recommendations.

## 12. CLOSING STATEMENT

CANONICO V3 transforms the standard from a **constraint satisfaction engine** into a **Knowledge & Coherence Operating System**. The system can now:

1. **Extract** knowledge from heterogeneous document corpora
2. **Map** concepts across different ontologies and vocabularies
3. **Detect** 33 types of inconsistencies spanning every dimension of coherence
4. **Trace** root causes across document boundaries
5. **Audit** entire corpora systematically
6. **Score** coherence at document, concept, and corpus levels
7. **Propose** minimal corrections with propagation analysis
8. **Fuse** external theoretical frameworks (ProofGraph, INPI)

Every new piece of information entering the corpus is now automatically extracted, mapped, validated against the existing knowledge base, and evaluated for coherence. No document is considered valid until it has been checked against the entire corpus.

CANONICO is no longer a standard. It is a **knowledge integrity system**.
